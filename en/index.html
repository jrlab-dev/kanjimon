FOR PARENTS

For learners and families.

Make time with kanji something to look forward to.
Kanjimon RPG brings reading, writing and word discovery into an adventure. English text guides you while Japanese voices and kanji keep you close to the language.

The whole adventure is free.

No account or app installation is required. Try the first adventure yourself, or explore it together with your child.

Which kanji will I encounter?

The game includes kanji taught in Japanese elementary-school grades 1–6. Start with simpler characters and discover compound words and four-character idioms as you progress. Take it at a pace that suits you.

Will I need to pay later?

No. All grade levels are free, including the content for grades 4–6.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. Play in a browser on your phone, tablet or computer. You can switch game sound on or off. The first visit may take a little longer to load images and audio, so a stable connection is recommended.

Can I continue later? Where is my progress saved?

Your progress is saved in the browser on your device. Use the same device and browser to continue. Progress does not automatically transfer to another device. Clearing site data may erase your progress, so avoid private browsing. You can export or restore a save file from the save settings.

Can I start if I am new to kanji?

The adventure starts with simpler kanji. Check readings and meanings in the collection or practice at the calligraphy school. English guidance and romanized readings help you get started. Younger players may benefit from playing with an adult.
